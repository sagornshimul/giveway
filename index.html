Questions

Good to know

Everything you need to know before you participate.

What do I get after I participate?
Two things. First, you're entered into the draw for one of 100 × $1,000 Walmart gift cards. Second, you'll be able to search rental listings — the full listings search unlocks immediately after participation, including filters for city, price, bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage.
Is it really free to participate?
Yes — 100% free. No purchase necessary. You never have to buy anything, and buying something won't improve your chances of winning. The giveaway is open to legal residents of the 50 U.S. states and D.C. who are 18 or older.
How many people win, and how much?
100 winners are selected, and each one receives a $1,000 Walmart gift card. That's a total prize pool of $100,000. Winners are chosen at random from all valid entries received during the entry window.
Do I have to participate to search the listings?
Yes. Participation in this year's annual giveaway is required for every visitor before the rental listings search unlocks. Once you've taken part, you'll have full search access to every available property — no further steps needed.
When does entry close?
Entry closes at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 31, 2026. The countdown at the top of this page shows exactly how much time is left. Entries received after that time won't be counted.
How will I know if I've won?
Winners are drawn at random after the entry window closes. We'll contact each winner by email at the address provided on the entry page, so make sure you enter an address you check regularly. If a winner can't be reached within the response window, an alternate winner may be drawn.
How many times can I enter?
One entry per person and per email address. Duplicate entries are removed before the draw, so there's no advantage to entering more than once — just make sure your single entry is submitted before the deadline.
